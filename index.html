Tips for a photo that gets accepted
  • Face the camera straight on, with a neutral expression and mouth closed.
  • Use even light with no shadows on your face or behind you.
  • Leave space above your head and include your shoulders.
  • Remove glasses, and keep hair away from your eyes.
  • Hold the camera at eye level, about an arm's length away (or have someone take it).

    How your photo stays private

    • Everything runs in your browser. The AI models (Google MediaPipe) are downloaded once from this site and run on your device with WebAssembly and your GPU.
    • There is no server. This is a static website with no upload code and nowhere to upload to.
    • Enforced by your browser. A Content-Security-Policy only allows connections back to this site's own files. Any attempt to send data elsewhere is blocked — including the AI library's built-in usage statistics.
    • Check it yourself. Turn off your Wi-Fi once the AI is ready — everything still works. Or open your browser's developer tools and watch the Network tab while you edit.
    • Nothing is stored. No cookies, no local storage of your photo. Closing the tab or “Start over” clears it.
    • Clean downloads. Saved photos are freshly generated pixels, without the location or camera metadata your original may contain.
